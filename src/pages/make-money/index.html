Before you apply

A quick verification checklist

  • Open the organisation’s official website yourself.
  • Check eligibility, location, dates and required documents.
  • Read what the funding or job actually pays for.
  • Never pay a person to secure an application or interview.
  • Do not share passwords, one-time codes or banking PINs.
Paid opportunities

Look for clear terms

Check whether a listing is employment, freelance work, a referral programme or a business partnership. Those are different arrangements and should have different terms.
