Learn · Courses

Study with a purpose.

There are plenty of courses online. The useful question is which one fits your current goal and what you will build with what you learn.

Before enrolling

Check the course first

  • Compare the syllabus with your goal.
  • Check when the material was last updated.
  • Look for exercises and a project you can finish.
  • Read the provider’s current price and access terms.
  • Start with free official documentation where possible.
After a lesson

Turn watching into practice

  • Write down the idea in your own words.
  • Recreate the example without copying.
  • Change one requirement and see what breaks.
  • Keep a short note of questions to revisit.
