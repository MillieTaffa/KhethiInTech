Learn · Career notes

Make the next step more manageable.

A few practical starting points for students exploring technology. Treat them as prompts to investigate, not a promise that one route works for everyone.

Build evidence

Make your learning visible

Choose a small problem, build a simple solution and write down what you learned. A clear explanation of your decisions can make a project easier to discuss.

Keep a routine

Make progress repeatable

Set a modest weekly goal. Keep a short list of questions, revisit the ones that still matter and leave time to practise without a tutorial open.

Ask for feedback

Bring a specific question

When you ask a mentor, teacher or community for help, share what you expected, what happened and what you already tried. Remove passwords, private data and other sensitive details.

Check the route

Read requirements yourself

Job, internship and scholarship criteria vary. Use an employer’s or funder’s own page to check current requirements, location and dates.

Keep learning

Find one useful next step

You do not have to map your entire career before you begin. Pick something you can learn, try it and keep the notes that help you move forward.
