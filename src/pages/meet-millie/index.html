About me

Hi, I’m Khethi.

I’m a software engineering student learning, building and sharing what I discover along the way.

Khethi in Tech is where I document that process: the questions, projects, useful resources and opportunities I find as I grow into technology.

Why KIT exists

Make the path easier to see

Technology can feel like a maze of acronyms, tools and advice. KIT brings my own learning notes together with clear starting points for other people who are finding their way in.

I’m sharing as a learner. The aim is to be useful and honest about what I know, what I’m still practising and where a resource comes from.
