# Khethi in Tech: Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

Khethi in Tech is a mobile-first, multi-page personal technology platform documenting Millie's journey through software engineering, technology, learning, teaching, and building.

The platform will combine personal storytelling, educational content, technical resources, project documentation, digital products, and opportunities for users to learn from and interact with the creator.

The project is intended to serve both as a public-facing platform and as a demonstration of software engineering, web development, documentation, content strategy, and product development skills.

---

## 2. Project Goals

The primary goals of Khethi in Tech are to:

* Document the creator's journey in technology.
* Help beginners understand technology concepts in accessible language.
* Provide practical resources for students and aspiring developers.
* Share useful developer cheatsheets and shortcuts.
* Showcase software projects and technical work.
* Share learning resources and course recommendations.
* Provide educational and career advice.
* Provide opportunities for users to purchase digital products.
* Provide opportunities for users to request one-on-one guidance.
* Build an audience around technology education and personal development.
* Establish a foundation for future monetisation through digital products, affiliate marketing, services, and advertising.
* Demonstrate professional software engineering practices through the development process.

---

## 3. Target Users

### Primary Users

* Students learning software engineering.
* Beginner programmers.
* Aspiring developers.
* People trying to enter the technology industry.
* Self-taught technology learners.
* Students looking for practical learning resources.
* Content creators interested in technology and productivity.

### Secondary Users

* Recruiters and potential employers.
* Other developers.
* Technology educators.
* Potential collaborators.
* Institutions interested in educational technology.
* Customers interested in digital products or one-on-one guidance.

## 4. Functional Requirements

### FR-001: Homepage

The system shall provide a homepage introducing Khethi in Tech and communicating its purpose.

The homepage shall provide clear pathways to major sections of the platform.

---

### FR-002: Navigation

The system shall provide navigation between the primary sections of the website.

Navigation shall be usable on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

---

### FR-003: Theme System

The system shall provide light and dark themes.

The system shall detect the user's system colour preference when no manual preference has been selected.

The user shall be able to manually switch themes.

---

### FR-004: Blog

The system shall provide a blog containing articles about technology, learning, software engineering, student life, career development, and related topics.

---

### FR-005: Cheatsheets

The system shall provide technical cheatsheets covering topics such as development tools, programming languages, Git, Linux, web development, and other relevant technologies.

Cheatsheets should prioritise practical information and quick reference.

---

### FR-006: Free Resources

The system shall provide users with access to free educational resources.

---

### FR-007: Course Recommendations

The system shall provide course recommendations, reviews, or educational resources.

Where applicable, affiliate relationships shall be disclosed.

---

### FR-008: Portfolio

The system shall provide a portfolio section showcasing projects created by the author.

Portfolio entries should provide relevant information about each project, including its purpose, technologies, development process, and available demonstrations or repositories where appropriate.

---

### FR-009: WTC Journey

The system shall provide content documenting the creator's journey as a WeThinkCode student.

This may include projects, lessons learned, experiences, tutorials, and weekly development updates.

---

### FR-010: Digital Products

The system shall provide a section for digital products.

Users shall be able to view available products and relevant product information.

Future versions may provide online purchasing and digital delivery.

---

### FR-011: One-on-One Services

The system shall provide information about available one-on-one guidance or educational services.

Users shall have a method of contacting the creator or requesting a session.

---

### FR-012: About / Meet Millie

The system shall provide an About section introducing the creator, their background, interests, journey, and purpose behind Khethi in Tech.

---

### FR-013: Search

The system should eventually provide search functionality allowing users to find articles, resources, cheatsheets, and other content.

Search functionality may be implemented after the initial release.

---

### FR-014: Contact

The system shall provide users with a method of contacting the creator.

---

### FR-015: Analytics

The system shall support analytics that can measure website usage and content performance.

Analytics should provide information such as:

* Page views.
* Traffic sources.
* Popular pages.
* Resource interactions.
* Product interactions.
* Relevant conversion events.

---

### FR-016: SEO

The system shall provide search-engine-optimised pages.

Each important page should support:

* A unique page title.
* A relevant meta description.
* Appropriate heading structure.
* Descriptive URLs.
* Internal links.
* Appropriate metadata.
* Search engine discovery.

---

### FR-017: Sitemap

The system shall provide a sitemap containing indexable public pages.

---

### FR-018: Robots Configuration

The system shall provide a robots.txt file defining crawler access where appropriate.

---

### FR-019: Responsive Design

The system shall provide an interface that works across:

* Mobile devices.
* Tablets.
* Desktop computers.

The design shall follow a mobile-first approach.

---

### FR-020: Accessibility

The system shall provide an accessible interface.

The website should support:

* Keyboard navigation.
* Visible focus states.
* Semantic HTML.
* Appropriate alternative text.
* Sufficient colour contrast.
* Accessible forms.
* Reduced-motion preferences where applicable.

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001: Performance

The website should load efficiently and minimise unnecessary resources.

Images and other assets should be appropriately optimised.

---

### NFR-002: Maintainability

The project shall use a clear and predictable file structure.

Reusable components and styles should be organised to reduce duplication.

---

### NFR-003: Scalability

The initial architecture should allow the project to grow into a larger content and educational platform without requiring an immediate complete rewrite.

---

### NFR-004: Security

The system shall not expose private credentials, API keys, passwords, or other sensitive configuration in publicly accessible source code.

External services shall be integrated using appropriate security practices.

---

### NFR-005: Compatibility

The website should function correctly in modern browsers.

---

### NFR-006: Usability

Users should be able to understand the purpose of the website and reach important content without unnecessary navigation.

---

### NFR-007: Documentation

Important architectural, design, deployment, security, and development decisions shall be documented.

---

### NFR-008: Version Control

The project shall use Git for version control.

Meaningful changes should be represented by clear commits.

---

## 6. SEO Requirements

The platform should:

* Use semantic HTML.
* Provide unique titles and descriptions.
* Use descriptive URLs.
* Provide internal linking.
* Provide a sitemap.
* Provide robots.txt.
* Use appropriate heading hierarchy.
* Optimise images.
* Provide descriptive alt text.
* Avoid duplicate content where practical.
* Support search engine indexing of appropriate public pages.

---

## 7. Monetisation Requirements

The platform should provide a foundation for multiple revenue streams.

Potential revenue streams include:

* Digital products.
* Affiliate marketing.
* One-on-one services.
* Sponsored content.
* Advertising.
* Future educational products.

Advertising shall not compromise the usability or accessibility of the platform.

---

## 8. Content Requirements

Content should be:

* Original.
* Useful.
* Accessible to the intended audience.
* Clearly structured.
* Factually responsible.
* Properly attributed where external sources are used.
* Written with the target audience in mind.

Educational content should explain technical concepts in understandable language.
